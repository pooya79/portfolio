# TorobRent decorative hero backdrop

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. These are decorative architecture images, never app screenshots or collection thumbnails. The real application captures remain the main visual.

Both variants are 1536 × 1024, optimized as WebP. Dark mode uses a lighting edit of the day scene to preserve the composition. See `src/pages/projects/torobrent/assets.ts` for the theme pair.

## Day generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: decorative background for a compact TorobRent portfolio hero, wide landscape 3:2 image, no interface.
Primary request: an elegant solarpunk residential neighborhood inspired by Tehran at the foot of the Alborz mountains, communicating a hopeful place to find a home.
Scene: planted contemporary apartment terraces, pale limestone, delicate rounded balconies with climbing vines, rooftop solar panels, small garden courtyard, distant mountains.
Composition: airy panoramic architectural visualization with most detail concentrated on the far right and lower edges; left third is very quiet pale atmospheric sky for HTML title overlay, center is softly hazy and low contrast because a REAL app screenshot will be placed over it. Nature and buildings should frame the screenshot rather than compete. No central focal tower. Eye-level perspective with depth.
Lighting: soft clear morning daylight, warm ivory and sage greenery, muted pale blue sky. Sophisticated natural architectural rendering, restrained, optimistic, subtle paper-like atmosphere. No fantasy megastructures.
Constraints: scenery only; no text, logos, watermarks, UI, devices, borders, mockup screens, or people. This is a backdrop, not a replacement for app imagery.

## Night edit prompt

Use case: lighting-weather.
Edit target: attached day architectural scene. Create its exactly matching night variant for the same website hero.
Change only lighting and atmosphere to a calm blue-hour night with a deep forest-green/blue sky, soft moonlight on the Alborz mountains, restrained warm amber light from a few apartment windows and garden paths. Keep every building, terrace, solar panel, plant, mountain, viewpoint, crop, and composition exactly the same. Preserve the very quiet left third and hazy low-contrast center for HTML content and a real app screenshot overlay. Elegant, subtle, believable residential architecture. Preserve image dimensions. No added text, logos, people, UI, devices, or border.
